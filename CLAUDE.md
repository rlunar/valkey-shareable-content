When working on presentations or slides, read `slides-skill.md` for the Valkey slide template (theme, type scale, layouts, geometry) and the programmatic editing workflow. The reusable pptx tooling and templates live in `slides/`.

Individual talks live in `sessions/<name>/`, each a self-contained Slidev project with its own README. The decks published to the slide-decks site are registered in `sessions/slidecks.yaml` and deployed by the CDK app in `infrastructure/cdk-infra/`.

See `README.md` for the full repository layout.

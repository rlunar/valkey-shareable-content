One-line: the site header — use it at the top of any valkey.io page recreation.

```jsx
<SiteHeader active="Documentation" starCount="21.4k" logoSrc="assets/img/valkey-horizontal.svg" />
```

Nav ink is ValkeyBlueAura; active/hovered items get a 3px bottom border above 1024px. Submenus open on hover with `0 2px 5px rgba(0,0,0,.2)`. Pass `logoSrc` relative to your page.

    Running the raffle

    1. Press Draw a winner or Space. Names shuffle for a few seconds, then one lands.
    2. Read the name out loud. The entry number and masked email help the winner recognise themselves.
    3. If they are not at the booth, press Not present, draw again or N. They leave the pool and the next name is drawn straight away.
    4. Everyone drawn is listed under Winners and saved in this browser, so a reload keeps the list. Export it from the menu when the raffle is over.
